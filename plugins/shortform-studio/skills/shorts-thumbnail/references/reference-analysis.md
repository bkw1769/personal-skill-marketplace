## What the hits share

Counts are from the 44 hits reviewed.

| Feature | Share | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| One subject in the middle third | almost all | Dam, wall, tower, slab or truck, filling 40-60% of the height. Background reads as context, not as a second subject. |
| Muted render, one accent colour | almost all | Grey, concrete, sea blue or dusk. The only saturated colour is the mark. |
| Red mark on the subject | about 70% | Glowing outline around the part that matters, thick arrows pointing in, a big red X, a red bar, a pin or ring. Usually one mark, at most three. |
| Cutaway or cross-section | about 25% | Slab with a ball on it, sliced hill, open wall. Shows the inside the title promises. |
| Big top text | about 25% | A number with its unit (`120,000 t`, `64t`, `125년`, `2017 M5.4`) or a 2-4 syllable keyword (`곡선`, `이음새`, `덩어리`, `수문 5칸`). Charcoal on light skies, white on dark. 2-8 characters. |
| Subtitle chunk in the lower middle | almost all | These thumbnails are frames of the video, so the caption of that moment shows. It works as a second hook (`되지 않나요?`, `생활 쓰레기.`). |
| Faces | rare | No faces or emotion. The object is the star. |

## What the low ones look like

Out of 12 thumbnails under 4만 views:

- None has a red mark.
- 8 use saturated, toy-like colours: blue gel water, cartoon people, money stacks.
- Several show a mid-action detail without context: a hand holding a glass, a pipe end.
- Only one has big top text.

## Caveats

- **Age effect.** The low group is mostly the newest uploads, so they have had less time to collect views. The contrast suggests the patterns matter, but it does not prove they cause the views.
- **Title effect.** The hits also have strong everyday or "Seoul" topics, and the title does part of the work.
- **No A/B data.** YouTube offers no A/B testing for Shorts thumbnails (as of 2026-10).

## Platform facts (checked 2026-10-19)

- Custom thumbnail upload for Shorts started on 2026-07-24 for YouTube Partner Program creators (desktop Studio). It is expanding to more creators.
- Everyone else picks one of three suggested frames on desktop, or any frame in the mobile app.
- So make **both**:
  - a 9:16 PNG for upload;
  - a frame in the video that looks like it: the hook shot with the keyword card. See SKILL.md step 6.
- Guides recommend 9:16. 1080x1920 is the minimum; 2160x3840 is the largest suggested size. Upload JPG or PNG under 50 MB.
