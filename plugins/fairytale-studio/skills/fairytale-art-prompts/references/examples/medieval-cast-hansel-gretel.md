```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: first character-design concept sheet for the children's fairy tale "헨젤과 그레텔", audience ages 6-10.

Input images: supplied local photos are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY, not edit targets. Image 1 creature-1.jpg: sparse imperfect black pen contours, watercolor paper grain, transparent overlapping pastel washes, simple friendly silhouettes. Image 2 creature-2.jpg: quirky long thin limbs, asymmetrical dot/oval eyes, tiny mouths, expressive naive handmade figures. Image 3 creature-3.jpg: economical facial expression and sketch-like character movement. Image 4 nature-2.jpg: airy blue/yellow/green translucent wash palette and abundant unpainted paper.

Translate exactly this handmade ink-and-watercolor sketchbook language, staying much closer to the rough naive reference drawings than to polished commercial digital cartoons. Wobbly thin black pen outlines with occasional thicker strokes, broken/open edges, translucent uneven watery color with pooling and small outside-line bleeds, visible off-white cold-press paper texture, a few faint construction marks. Minimal two-dimensional drawing: no realistic lighting or shading, no airbrush, no glossy eyes, no vector-perfect edges, no generic chibi/anime style. Expressive primitive bean/oval body shapes with thin drawn arms and legs, slightly asymmetrical dot or small oval eyes, minimal eyebrows and tiny mouths. Clothes have only a few essential lines. Fewer lines than conventional commercial illustration; abundant unpainted paper. Render cleanly as a scanned artwork, without camera shadows, table edges or photographic distortion.

Setting: storybook medieval Europe, simplified for young children. Clothing is made of plain wool and linen in soft natural dyes: long belted tunics and kirtles, hooded short capes, aprons, simple soft leather shoes or ankle boots. Shapes are simple and rounded; show period only through a few telling details (hoods, belts with small pouches, veils, simple circlets), never through dense ornament.

Large LANDSCAPE artist's model sheet on off-white watercolor paper, spacious, generous whitespace, no boxes, cards, borders or design-app UI. A small neat handwritten Korean title in a clear top band; no subject enters that band. Every figure is complete head-to-toe and stands apart with clear paper between it and its neighbours, even within a pair. Each caption sits just below its figure without touching it. Exactly the captions listed, no other text. All Korean captions accurate and legible.
Composition: eight clearly separated groups in two rows of four. Title: "헨젤과 그레텔 · 캐릭터 시안". Captions in order —
Top row: "헨젤", "그레텔", "나무꾼 아버지", "새엄마".
Bottom row: "마녀", "흰 오리", "과자 집", "비례 비교".
Small simple handwritten color swatches under the title if space allows.

1 헨젤: brave clever 9-year-old boy, round head with messy straw-blond hair, knee-length belted leaf-green wool tunic, warm ochre short hood with a little tail, brown hose and soft ankle boots, small cloth pouch of white pebbles at his belt, slightly asymmetrical dot eyes. Full-body 3/4 pose dropping one pebble behind him with a sly little smile.
2 그레텔: gentle quick-witted 7-year-old girl, light-brown hair in one short plait, ankle-length watery coral kirtle, linen-cream small apron, light sky-blue short hooded cape, soft shoes. Full-body pose holding her brother's sleeve, curious and a bit worried, tiny eyebrows raised.
3 나무꾼 아버지: kind tired adult woodcutter, tall and thin with a long oval face and short scruffy beard, pale brown short belted tunic with rolled sleeves, woad-blue hood pushed back, leather belt pouch, small hand axe resting on his shoulder handle-down as a tool. Soft sad eyebrows, warm gentle expression.
4 새엄마: thin stiff adult woman, tall narrow silhouette, pale lilac long kirtle, linen headscarf tied back, arms folded, pinched little mouth; strict and grumpy but not frightening.
5 마녀: short round comic old woman, big hooked nose drawn with one line, dark plum long robe, moss-green shawl, soft pointed hood, crooked walking stick, one tooth showing in a sneaky grin; mischievous and silly, never scary.
6 흰 오리: friendly white duck with a simple bean-shaped body, orange beak and feet, dot eyes, in the tiny-creature manner of naive handmade drawings; no human clothes.
7 과자 집: small crooked half-timbered cottage made of gingerbread, steep roof of biscuit tiles, pink and white icing drips along the eaves, round sugar-glass windows, candy-stick chimney with a smoke curl, lollipop flowers by the door; cosy and inviting.
8 비례 비교: tiny same Hansel and same Gretel standing beside a larger thumbnail of the same gingerbread house, emphasize that the door is twice their height; neutral grounding pen line only.

Palette: turquoise/sky blue, warm yellow ochre, leaf green, watery pink/coral, lilac accents and pale brown, unified across all characters, plus muted moss green and woad blue.

Constraints: character concepts only, not story scenes. No photorealism, no 3D, no cut-paper collage, no digital gradients, no polished vector, no complex realistic shading, no glossy cartoon finish. No ancient Greek or Roman tunics, togas, sandals or crested helmets; no Renaissance ruffs or slashed sleeves; no full gothic plate armor; no Victorian dresses, corsets or top hats; no sparkly modern princess ball gowns; no buttons-and-zippers modern clothes, glasses, wristwatches or plastic. No oven scenes, cages, blood, injuries or frightening faces. Eyes are simple dots or tiny ovals, no highlights, no lashes.
```

Notes:
- The story's oven and cage are deliberately left out of concept sheets; they belong to scene staging, with the era's audience notes applied.
- Signature colors do not overlap: Hansel green/ochre, Gretel coral/sky blue, father pale brown/woad blue, stepmother lilac, witch plum/moss.
