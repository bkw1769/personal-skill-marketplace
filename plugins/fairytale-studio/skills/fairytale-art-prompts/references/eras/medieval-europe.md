# Era: medieval-europe (중세 유럽 · 동화풍)

Status: drafted, not yet proven by an approved sheet.

## Scope

The storybook Middle Ages of Grimm, Perrault and Andersen tales: roughly 12th–15th century Western and Central Europe, simplified for children. Villages, forests, mills and castles with round towers. It is a fairy-tale picture of the period, not a reconstruction: keep shapes simple and friendly, and pick from the libraries below instead of inventing period detail.

Fits: 헨젤과 그레텔, 브레멘 음악대, 잠자는 숲속의 공주, 라푼젤, 백설공주, 장화 신은 고양이, 피리 부는 사나이, 개구리 왕자, 룸펠슈틸츠헨.
Does not fit: Greek or Roman myths (e.g. 트로이), Arabian Nights, Korean or East Asian tales. Those need their own era files.

## ERA BLOCK (paste verbatim)

```text
Setting: storybook medieval Europe, simplified for young children. Clothing is made of plain wool and linen in soft natural dyes: long belted tunics and kirtles, hooded short capes, aprons, simple soft leather shoes or ankle boots. Shapes are simple and rounded; show period only through a few telling details (hoods, belts with small pouches, veils, simple circlets), never through dense ornament. Architecture, when present, is half-timbered cottages with steep thatched or shingle roofs, crooked stone towers with conical roofs, a small round-towered castle, a wooden water mill, a stone well, forest paths.
```

## Costume library

Pick one line per character and adapt the colors to the character bible.

| Role | Costume line |
|---|---|
| Village child (boy) | knee-length belted wool tunic, hose, short hood with a little tail, soft ankle boots |
| Village child (girl) | ankle-length simple kirtle, small apron, short hooded cape, plait or loose short hair, soft shoes |
| Woodcutter / farmer | short belted tunic with rolled sleeves, hood pushed back, leather belt pouch, small hand axe kept on the shoulder handle-down (tool, not weapon) |
| Village woman / mother | long kirtle with apron, linen headscarf or veil tied back |
| Miller / baker | flour-dusted tunic and apron, soft round cap |
| Merchant / innkeeper | rounded body, long tunic with a wide belt, coin pouch, soft brimmed hat |
| King | long robe in one strong color, short fur-trimmed cape drawn as a simple wavy edge, simple gold circlet crown with three small points |
| Queen | long fitted gown, flowing sleeves, veil with a thin gold circlet |
| Prince / princess | short tunic or simple gown, light cape, thin circlet; youthful, no glamour pose |
| Knight / guard | simple surcoat with one large plain heraldic shape (star, circle, stripe) over mail suggested by a few loops, rounded open helmet, wooden or blunt-looking sword kept sheathed, round or kite shield |
| Monk / friar | brown hooded robe with rope belt, round friendly face |
| Minstrel / piper | parti-colored tunic split into two colors, pointed hood, small pipe or lute |
| Wise woman / witch | long dark-plum or moss robe, pointed soft hood, crooked walking stick, big nose drawn with one line; comic, never frightening |
| Fairy / magical helper | small figure in a petal-like short dress, thin wings drawn as two loops, pale glow suggested by bare paper around it |
| Animals | talking animals wear at most one human item (boots, hat, scarf); bodies stay simple bean shapes in the style of `veg.jpg` |

## Architecture and locations

| Location | Lines |
|---|---|
| Village | half-timbered cottages leaning slightly, steep thatched roofs, small round windows, smoke curls, a stone well, a cart with big wooden wheels |
| Forest | tall trees with round watery canopies, mushrooms, a winding path, a few berry bushes, gaps of bare paper between trees |
| Castle | small and friendly: round towers with conical blue or red roofs, crooked walls, a wooden drawbridge, pennant flags |
| Mill | wooden water mill with a big wheel, a little stream drawn with horizontal ink ripples |
| Interior | big stone hearth with a hanging pot, wooden table and stools, candles, a spinning wheel |
| Market | striped canvas stalls, baskets of vegetables, barrels |

## Props

Lanterns, candles in holders, spinning wheel, wooden spoons and bowls, round loaves, baskets, barrels, scrolls with a wax seal, keys on rings, quill and inkpot, wooden buckets, apples, a simple wooden toy horse.

## Palette tilt

Add to the style palette at most two of: muted moss green, madder red, woad blue, undyed linen cream. Royals may carry one saturated color (royal blue or deep red); keep villagers in softer natural dyes so rank reads at a glance.

## Anachronism negatives (paste into Constraints)

```text
No ancient Greek or Roman tunics, togas, sandals or crested helmets; no Renaissance ruffs or slashed sleeves; no full gothic plate armor; no Victorian dresses, corsets or top hats; no sparkly modern princess ball gowns; no buttons-and-zippers modern clothes, glasses, wristwatches or plastic; no East Asian costume unless the story asks for it.
```

## Audience notes

Swords stay sheathed, axes are tools, dragons and witches are comic. No dungeons, chains, executions, blood or skulls. Wolves and witches can be mischievous but must look soft and a little silly.
